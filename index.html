side projects

01

Multi-Threaded Search Engine

A six-person build of a search engine from the ground up, on a C/C++ standard library we wrote ourselves — hash tables and sockets included. A manager split the web by domain across 48 worker machines, each crawling, parsing and indexing its own shard at up to ~3,800 pages a second. Queries fan out to every worker, which ranks its own top 10 for the manager to merge. Crawled 191M pages; I built much of the manager and the HTML parser.

Weather Balloon Payload

Probably my favorite project to date. A five-person team went from learning to solder to launching sensors into the atmosphere. We added a gyroscope and barometer to measure wind shear by altitude — the turbulence airliners try to avoid. Won “Best Overall Design.”
